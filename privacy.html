Privacy Policy — Tradestack Marketing Agent

This application runs entirely in your browser. All business data (profile, products, content, tokens) is stored locally on your device, encrypted at rest. Nothing is sold or shared.

Facebook data

If you connect a Facebook Page, the app receives your Page name and an access token via official Facebook Login. The token is encrypted, stored only on your device, and used solely to publish posts you approve and to read engagement on your own posts.

User data deletion

To delete all data: open the app → Settings → "Delete organisation data", or clear the browser's site data for this domain. This permanently removes everything stored locally, including encrypted Facebook tokens. You may also disconnect a Page at any time under Social Accounts.
